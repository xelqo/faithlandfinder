# Sources for rules.yaml (checked 2026-11-06)

## Official
- **CA Dept. of Insurance, "FAQ: Safer from Wildfires Regulation" (Sept 2026)**: `sources/cdi_faq.pdf`
  https://www.insurance.ca.gov/01-consumers/105-type/95-guides/03-res/upload/faq-safer-from-wildfire-regulation.pdf
  The 11-item discount list ("Every action under Safer from Wildfires will qualify you for an insurance discount"),
  risk-score disclosure and appeal rights, FAIR Plan discounts, Firewise verification.
- **PRC 4291 (defensible space law)**: https://law.justia.com/codes/california/code-prc/division-4/part-2/chapter-3/section-4291
  Branches within 10 ft of a chimney; dead branches over roofs/walls; leaves off roofs and gutters. Applies in
  designated fire-hazard areas. Zones 0–5 / 5–30 / 30–100 ft.
- **CAL FIRE**: grass cut to max 4 inches (fire.ca.gov/dspace; readyforwildfire.org blocks scripts, so confirm in a browser)
  https://readyforwildfire.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Defensible-Space-and-the-Law-Factsheet-Revisions-Jan-2024.pdf
- **Board of Forestry inspection form LE-100**: https://bof.fire.ca.gov/media/3izdfb2j/calfire_le100a_sample.pdf

## Zone 0 status (the 5-ft rule as LAW). Not in force.
- Board of Forestry adopted emergency Zone 0 regulations Aug 19, 2026 (8-0). Not operative until OAL approves.
  One tracker reports the Board withdrew the package from OAL on Sept 8, 2026; an Oct 2026 update says "not in effect".
  https://bbklaw.com/resources/la090926-california-board-of-forestry-adopts-emergency-zone-0-regulations-under-ab-3074
  https://cadefensiblespace.com/rule-status/
  https://www.flycre.com/blog/zone-0-california-guide.html
- Adopted text (per secondary sources): no bark/wood-chip mulch, firewood, dead leaves within 5 ft; 5-ft noncombustible
  section where a fence attaches; limited non-woody plants allowed.
- The INSURANCE discount for a "5-foot ember-resistant zone" exists regardless of the Zone 0 law.

## Discount size
- Commissioner's office (2025, via United Policyholders): 4% to 40%+ of premium depending on risk. Varies by insurer.
  https://uphelp.org/california-will-require-insurers-to-reward-consumers-for-wildfire-mitigations/
