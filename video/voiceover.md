# Voiceover: Faith Land Finder (~63 s)

Read at a relaxed pace (about 150 words a minute). Each block starts when its scene starts; a short pause
between blocks is fine. Times are in `timing.json` and can be moved to match the recording.

**[0:00 · price]**
In Orange County, the median home costs one point four seven million dollars. Only fifteen percent of households can afford one.

**[0:06.5 · land]**
For nonprofits building affordable homes, the biggest line on the bill is land. And coastal SoCal has almost no vacant land left.

**[0:13 · churches]**
But look closer. In the Santa Ana area alone there are fifty-one places of worship, and many sit on acres of open land and parking.

**[0:21 · the law]**
Since 2024, a California law called SB 4 lets churches build one hundred percent affordable homes on their land, approved by right. But every site has to pass a stack of tests.

**[0:30 · demo]**
Faith Land Finder runs those tests for you. Type in a church's address. It pulls the parcel, the buildings, zoning, and the fire, flood and landslide maps. Then Claude reads the satellite image. Here it sees a parking lot and a ball field, and flags who might be using it. It weighs whether the congregation owns the land, scores the site, and writes a brief for the first meeting with the church. Every fact links to its source.

**[0:54 · outro]**
Fifty-one places of worship, fifteen sites checked, about seven hundred eighty homes. The next affordable home might already have a steeple.
