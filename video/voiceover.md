# Voiceover: Faith Land Finder v3 (~48 s)

Read as one take with a short pause between paragraphs. The scenes stretch to fit your recording.

**[1 · the problem]**
In Orange County, the median home costs one point four seven million dollars, and the biggest cost of building affordable homes is the land.

**[2 · the map]**
But look at the map. In the Santa Ana area alone there are fifty-one places of worship, many on acres of parking and open land. Since 2024, a law called SB 4 lets churches build one hundred percent affordable homes on that land, approved by right. And they don't have to sell: a nonprofit can lease the land, pay to build, and the church keeps its sanctuary and earns lease income.

**[3 · the demo]**
Faith Land Finder checks whether a site qualifies. Enter a church's address, and Claude reads the satellite image and public records, scores the site, and writes a brief for the first meeting.

**[4 · outro]**
In Santa Ana alone, eleven of the fifteen sites we checked pass the hazard tests. That's room for about six hundred eighty homes, on paper. Scale that across Southern California, and the next affordable home might already have a steeple.
